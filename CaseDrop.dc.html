MOST-READ JUSTIFICATION · DEFEND
“The badge still worked and the deactivation was scheduled for the morning. An employer that leaves access operational has not personally communicated anything.”
REVIEW CASE
Why both sides were defensible
DEFEND
Revocation requires communication that actually reaches the person. An unread email sent at 17:03, paired with a badge the employer left live and a guard who admitted her, supports the view that permission had not yet been withdrawn in any operative sense.
CHALLENGE
Termination and an express instruction not to return were both sent before entry. Entering a restricted records room at 20:14 is not explained by retrieving belongings, and a person who is told to stay out bears some duty to read what an employer sends.
STRONGER REASONING
The strongest submissions on both sides separated the entry from the records room, and treated them as two questions rather than one.
